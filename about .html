About Bikaner Sweets

Founded in the royal city of Bikaner, Rajasthan, Bikaner Sweets has been serving authentic Indian mithai for more than 70 years. We follow traditional recipes passed down through generations, using only pure desi ghee, fresh khoya, and premium ingredients.


Our commitment to quality, purity, and taste has made us a preferred choice for festivals, weddings, and corporate gifting.
